# Creative quality gates — detailed rules

Reference for the deterministic checks, the post-render image QA and the eval gates added by
the 2026-10-07 creative-quality work (PRs #263–#280), plus later additions: person casting
(2026-10-10), the trend situation validator (#319) and the timely slot (#320). Opt-in
rating learning can only *tighten* some of these checks (`rating_strictness`; see the
Rating learning bullet in `CLAUDE.md`). `CLAUDE.md` keeps the summary; this
note keeps the calibrated details that matter when you change a heuristic. Plan:
[docs/plans/2026-10-07-creative-quality.md](../plans/2026-10-07-creative-quality.md).
Research: [docs/research/2026-10-07-creative-quality.md](../research/2026-10-07-creative-quality.md).

All of these are deliberately **conservative**: a false failure costs a revision round or a
re-render (against the ~2 RPM image quota) and erodes trust in the gate, so each rule was
tuned on live runs to flag only clear problems (false-positive fixes: #268).

## Concept guard + concept gate (`creative_agent/concept_guard.py`, `text_match.py`)

"Missing" (motif, product and brand cue alike) is token overlap, not a substring (`creative_agent/text_match.mentions`: the full phrase, or ≥60% of its content tokens, plural/accent-insensitive; size tokens and a size's container ("16oz can") ignored, a packaging head noun ("iPhone case") required; a single-token phrase needs its full core ("Pixel 9"); with a brand, the brand plus every distinctive token (a digit or all caps in the original phrase) counts regardless of the ratio, so "PRS SE CE24" names "SE CE24 Electric Guitar" (packaging head noun still required; the brand-less motif check never uses this rule); brand-anchored — a partial product/brand-cue match must include a brand token; separately, in the copy gate the brand alone never names the product (since 2026-10-12, matching the judge's `product_named` gate)); intangible products (subscription/app/service/insurance…) get a softer branded-screen/logo cue, skipped when the brand is mentioned. `concept_gate` then runs `concept_guard.concept_issues` (deliberately conservative string heuristics — only a double-quoted span with a text cue ≤6 words before it counts as in-image text (2026-10-08 added quote/quoted/quotes/phrase/wording/inscription/inscribed; the often-descriptive painted/neon/bearing/displays/displaying count only directly before the quote, so "painted in warm \"golden hour\" tones" stays descriptive), idioms like "sign of", "title of", "poster style", "vibe reads" excluded; only copy-matching text concepts count toward the cap (mismatches get their own issue); meme/comic concepts are exempt (style contains "meme" or a meme/comic family, or the prompt says meme caption/speech bubble|balloon/thought bubble/comic panel/top|bottom text/Impact font); a bare "centred" needs a subject/composition word, and the centred-hero check skips "off-centre", "centred between", "camera centred on", "… third" placements and sentences about text/logos) and routes flagged concepts to one bounded `visual_concept_fixer` round (`CONCEPT_REVISION_ROUNDS`, default 1, clamped 0–2).

## Post-render image QA (`creative_agent/image_qa.py`)

`image_tools.generate_image` inspects every render with one structured vision call (`creative_agent/image_qa.py` `inspect_image` → `ImageQAResult`, model `IMAGE_QA_MODEL`, default the worker model, temperature 0, global client with HTTP retry + the shared timeout). **Conservative by design** (every false failure costs a re-render against the ~2 RPM image quota; calibrated on live renders): the QA instruction includes the concept's `image_generation_prompt` (capped at 1500 chars) as "What the image was asked to show"; `product_visible` accepts a recognisable partial/close-up view (an intangible product or abstract motif counts when the prompt's depiction is present); `motif_visible` asks only that the motif is visible (not tiny or hidden in the background) and recognisable as the trend, stylised/abstract/symbolic references counting, like the judge's loosened `trend_motif_visible` — a prominent motif is required only under the `trend_unclear` rating strictness flag (`PROMINENT_MOTIF_RULE`; restored 2026-10-12 after #330 had made prominence always-on); `gibberish_text` covers only prominent, readable-size garbled lettering (tiny/blurred background text and stylised renderings of the brand's own logo don't count; meme/comic slang spelling is exempt); `unrequested_logos` only clearly recognisable marks of brands other than the campaign brand, the product's maker, any brand named in the prompt/its quoted text, the `brand_cue` and a logo reference image. The pure rule `qa_failed_rules(result, concept, target_product=…)` fails an image on: product or trend motif not visible (skipped when `target_product` / `trend_motif` is empty), a malformed product (`product_malformed`, default `False` so older payloads parse: mirrored/reversed, parts missing/duplicated/misplaced, warped or melted; skipped without a product; not a critical rule; correction "render the product with its correct real-world shape and orientation"; added 2026-10-08 after a backwards guitar passed), an off-model product (`product_off_model`, default `False`, asked only with a product: drawn as a generic object whose recognisable silhouette or shape is replaced, e.g. a plain block or generic bottle; normal stylisation that keeps it recognisable — flat colours, comic inking, simplified detail — and a missing, simplified or unreadable label do not count (prompts often keep readable text out of the image); skipped without a product; not critical; correction "render the product with its real silhouette, shape and label, even in a stylised look", always restated; added 2026-10-12 after a Comic panel turned an amber dropper bottle into a plain block that the judge failed on `avoid_respected`), gibberish text, an unrequested logo, noticeable artifacts (softened from "severe" 2026-10-08; the rule name stays `severe visual artifacts`), unsafe content, or required in-image text not exact/legible — `expected_text` (= `concept_guard.in_image_quotes`) is authoritative; the model's `text_expected` only counts for a meme/comic concept whose prompt quotes text. A missing `brand_cue` is advisory. **Prevention:** every render prompt (re-renders too) ends with `image_tools.no_other_logos_line` ("No logos, brand marks or trademarks except those of <brand>."; without a brand, "No logos, brand marks or trademarks.") — since 2026-10-12 always, no longer only under the `unwanted_logo` rating-strictness flag, because an unrequested third-party logo was the top re-render cause (5 of 13 re-renders across 20 runs since 2026-10-09). Person-swap edits still add it only under that flag (the edit must keep the original image unchanged). A failing image is re-rendered at most `IMAGE_QA_MAX_RERENDERS` times (default 1, clamped 0–2) and at most `IMAGE_QA_MAX_RERENDERS_PER_RUN` times across the whole call (default 2, clamped 0–8; when hit, the image's `image_qa__issues` entry gets "(re-render budget reached)"), with `image_qa.correction_text` appended (the model's problem statements with quote characters stripped, each ≤120 chars — fixed rule phrases when a text rule failed — always ending "Do not add any new text to the image."; same references / aspect ratio, through the quota-paced backoff, whose sync genai call runs via `asyncio.to_thread`). Keep-decision: compare (critical failures — `unsafe`, `unrequested_logos` — then total) lexicographically, ties → latest, and never keep a retry that introduces `unsafe`; only the kept image is uploaded. `IMAGE_QA_ENABLED=false` is the kill switch (single render, no QA calls). All four knobs live on `BaseAgentConfiguration` and ship via `deploy_agent.py` `ENV_VAR_DICT` (read at import: Agent Engine bakes in the deployer's env). Fail-open: a QA error keeps the render (`qa: None`) and lists the concept in `image_qa__unavailable` — deliberately not an `__issues` key, so `collect_degradation_warnings` (which only surfaces `__issues`/`__retry_exhausted`) doesn't report it as an unresolved issue. Results: `state["generated_images"] = {concept_name: {gcs_uri, artifact_key, attempts, qa}}` (`qa` = verdict fields + `passed` + `failures` (the model's problem statements, else rule names), None when disabled/unavailable; `_generated_artifact_keys` unchanged); still-failing images go to `image_qa__issues` (→ `collect_degradation_warnings`); the HTML gallery shows a per-image "Image check" line and the results proof detail an "Image check" row.

## Eval gates (`creative_eval/`)

The judge answers a judge-only schema (`AdCopyJudgeOutput` / `VisualJudgeOutput`: `gates` required, min 1; no `overall_score`/`passed`/`gates_passed`/`advisory`/`image_judged` — code maps it to the report models and computes those). Gate prompts: presence gates pass only when clearly present, violation gates (`avoid_respected`, carryable `mandatories_met`, `no_visual_defects`, `trend_risks_respected`) fail only on a clear violation, not-applicable passes with the reason in the note. Gates are normalised in code (`normalize_gates`: none of the expected gates reported → one failed `gates_reported` gate, "judge returned no gates"; a partial omission passes as "not checked" and adds a report warning "N checks not reported by the judge"; without a brief the brief-dependent gates pass with note "no brief" and `report.brief_used=False`). The visual judge sees the **rendered image** (`generated_images[concept].gcs_uri` as a `gs://` `Part`; the prompt is only intent; the image-QA verdict is a hint line) (fetched by the Vertex AI service agent; cross-project buckets need it granted `storage.objectViewer`) — no image, or an image read error (403/404, or a 400 mentioning the image/URI/file/permission; other 4xx are normal judge failures), falls back to judging the prompt (`image_judged=False`; the fallback adds a report warning).

**Current gate set** (`creative_eval/dimensions.py`, `JUDGE_VERSION = "2026-10-12"`; checked 2026-10-12):

| Kind | Gates (prompt/report order) |
|---|---|
| Ad copy (6) | `delivers_proposition`, `product_named`, `uses_reason_to_believe`, `mandatories_met`, `avoid_respected`, `trend_risks_respected` |
| Visual (8) | `product_visible`, `trend_motif_visible`, `text_correct`, `brand_cue_present` (advisory, never blocks), `avoid_respected`, `no_visual_defects`, `trend_risks_respected`, `person_depicted_respectfully` (only judged for a cast concept, else passed "no person cast") |

Brief-dependent gates (`BRIEF_GATES`): `delivers_proposition`, `uses_reason_to_believe`, `mandatories_met`, `avoid_respected`, `trend_risks_respected`. A creative `passed` only when its normalised score ≥ `passing_threshold` (0.7) **and** every non-advisory gate passed. Bump `JUDGE_VERSION` to the change's date whenever gates, their wording, scoring/pass rules or the judge model change (calibration counts only current-version ratings; [judge-calibration.md](judge-calibration.md)). The timely slot (below) has no judge gate yet; its checks are all pre-eval.

## Person casting (2026-10-10, plan `docs/plans/2026-10-09-person-reference.md` PR 2)

Only for runs whose `person_reference` names an active consent (checked by the api at kick-off and resume). Calibration and the defaults below: [person-reference-calibration.md](person-reference-calibration.md).

- **Prompts:** the casting rules (`prompts.PERSON_CASTING_RULES`) reach the art director, drafter, critic, finalizer, fixer and interactive reviser only through state `person_casting_rules` (set at state init when a person reference exists, else `""`; `{person_casting_rules?}` ends their last instruction line), so runs without a person get byte-identical instructions; image QA likewise adds its person line only for cast concepts.
- **Resume after a revoke:** the resume clears `person_reference` / `person_reference_available` / `person_casting_rules` and continues with the run note "Person reference consent was revoked; continued without the person" (a consent-store error is a 503).
- **Shares (plan PR 4):** a cast concept (`generated_images[c].cast`) is shared only when its `consent_id` is an active consent of the share owner with `allow_public_share`; a slate share skips the others (`skipped` in the response) and naming one is a 400 `person_not_shareable`. Revoking the consent revokes those shares and deletes the cast renders and variants recorded in `person_renders`.
- **Casting guard** (`concept_guard.enforce_person_casting`, first step of `ensure_trend_and_product_callback`, so after the finalizer, the fixer and interactive's reviser, and again in `image_tools.generate_image` before the photo is attached): clears `casts_person_reference` (warning + a "(Not cast: …)" suffix on `person_casting_reason`) when no person reference is available, `canonical_style(visual_style)` is not in `PERSON_SAFE_STYLES` (default: Candid 35mm film photo, Photoreal / editorial, Cinematic film still, 3D character render, Comic panel, 2D flat / vector cartoon, Collage / mixed-media), the prompt (with "the person in the person reference image" removed) has no human-subject word (person, man, woman, hero, model, athlete, musician, player, guitarist, … portrait, selfie), or more than `MAX_CAST_CONCEPTS` (default 2; 0 = off) concepts cast (the first N in concept order are kept). Any non-`True` flag normalises to `False`. A kept cast without "the person in the person reference image" in its prompt gets " The hero is the person in the person reference image." appended; a cleared cast has that wording replaced by "a person". Not part of `concept_issues` (no fix round: clearing is the fix).
- **Render** (`image_tools`, `person_render.py`): cast concepts get the photo as the last reference part ("Reference image N (person): Cast this exact person as the hero: keep their face shape, skin tone, hair, eye colour, apparent age and distinguishing features; change only pose, clothing, expression and lighting as described. Do not beautify, slim or alter their body.") and `ImageConfig(person_generation="ALLOW_ADULT")`. A block (`person_block_reason`: `prompt_feedback.block_reason` → `prompt_blocked:<reason>`; finish reason `SAFETY` / `PROHIBITED_CONTENT` / `IMAGE_SAFETY` / `IMAGE_PROHIBITED_CONTENT` / `BLOCKLIST` / `SPII` → lower-cased; no image → `no_image`; a non-retryable 4xx → `request_rejected:<code>`) or an unreadable photo (`photo_unavailable`) renders the concept again without the person and records `person_reference_rejected[concept]` plus a `person_reference__issues` note. `generated_images[concept]["cast"]` is set on every record of a run with a person reference (`consent_id` too when cast); QA re-renders of a cast concept keep the person part and config.
- **Image QA likeness:** for a cast concept the photo is attached after the render and the instruction asks "Is the hero clearly the same person as the reference image?" (`person_cast`, `person_likeness`, `person_distorted`, `person_as_public_figure`; older payloads parse with `None` / `None` / `False` / `False`, and a stored `person_marks` key from 2026-10-12 is ignored; `person_as_public_figure` is stored for cast concepts only). Rules `person likeness lost` (`person_likeness is False`; unknown is not a failure), `person distorted`, `person shown as a public figure` (the hero is clearly dressed or presented as a specific real public figure; ordinary fan clothing is not — casting on real-person trends is allowed since 2026-10-12 under the casting rules' identity guardrails), all only for concepts with `casts_person_reference`, none critical; their correction phrase is always appended (it restates the likeness instruction). A fallback (uncast) render is inspected as an uncast concept. There is no tattoo/skin-marks check: a `person marked` rule (added 2026-10-12) was removed the same day because the consented reference person really has face and neck tattoos, which the image model reproduced faithfully.
- **Judge gate** `person_depicted_respectfully` (visual, blocking): "The cast person is shown respectfully: no mockery, sexualisation, injury, or demeaning situations, and no altered body or added attributes beyond the brief." The prompt shows "Person Cast: yes|no" from `generated_images[concept].cast`; `normalize_gates(..., person_cast=False)` passes the gate with note "no person cast" whatever the judge said (`PERSON_GATES`), so only real casts can fail it. `JUDGE_VERSION` → `2026-10-10`. Since `2026-10-12` a cast concept's judge request also attaches the consented person's reference photo (`state["person_reference"].uri`, the engine's `person-refs/` shape in the configured bucket, `creative_eval.evaluate.person_photo_uri`) after the render, labelled "Reference photo of the consented person cast as the hero:", with an instruction that the person's own appearance in it (tattoos, piercings, glasses, hairstyle, facial hair, skin marks, clothing style) is correct and intended — never a visual defect, hallucination, added attribute or disrespectful depiction — and that likeness and respect are judged against it (a live run's judge had failed `no_visual_defects` + this gate on the cast person's real face/neck tattoos). No usable photo → judged without it; an unreadable photo (image 4xx) is retried with the render only. The URI is never logged or stored on the report.
- **Personalised variants: edit mode + composition check** (2026-10-11, plan `docs/plans/2026-10-11-faithful-person-swaps.md` PR 1; spike [person-swap-edit-mode.md](person-swap-edit-mode.md)): a variant edits the run's original render instead of re-generating the scene (`render_concept.render_edit`, `person_edit.EDIT_INSTRUCTION`: "replace only the main person … keep everything else in image 1 exactly as it is"; original before the person photo, each labelled). Image QA then gets the original too (`inspect_image(..., original_image=)`, attached last after the label "Original ad (before the person was replaced):") and asks: "Apart from the person, does the edited image keep the original's layout, text, graphic elements (count and direction), props and product? Set composition_changed and list concrete differences." → `composition_changed` / `composition_differences` (default `False` / `[]`, old payloads parse; without an original both are always cleared, so base renders never fail it and their stored QA record doesn't carry the fields). Rule `composition changed`, **not critical**; its correction restates "keep everything except the person exactly as in image 1" plus up to 5 differences (quotes and braces stripped, each capped at 120 chars), and the displayed failures list the differences. A failing likeness or composition repeats the **edit of the original** with the correction appended (same bound as base renders). Variants only: engine renders and the render-parity golden are unchanged.

## Brand history (`creative_agent/brand_history.py`)

`load_brand_history` (function node, no LLM) runs in `combined_research_pipeline`'s START fan-out next to the two planners and feeds `research_join`. It reads the brand's latest `BRAND_HISTORY_RUNS` (default 5, clamped 0–20) `creative_evals` rows (parameterised SELECT, case-insensitive brand match) plus their eval-report JSON (configured bucket only, 5 MB cap), and writes `brand_history` — a brace-free ≤120-word note (recent styles, strongest styles/copy tones, recurring weak dimensions, often-failed gates) read via `{brand_history?}` by the brief writer and art director. Unless `visual_style_preference` is set, it re-draws `style_shortlist` without the recently used styles (`pick_style_shortlist(exclude=...)`, 2/3/1 strata kept); with opt-in rating learning a style the team rated well is not excluded for being recent, and a poorly rated one always is (`shortlist_exclusions`). Fail-soft: 10 s timeout in a worker thread, any error → `""`; `BRAND_HISTORY_ENABLED=false` skips it (no query). Eval report entries now carry `visual_style` / `angle_id` (code-set, `""` in old reports); angle ids are per-brief so only styles and copy tones are aggregated. The `creative_ratings` table is not joined (no clean run key). Only allowlisted values reach the prompts: report styles are mapped to canonical `STYLE_GROUPS` families (`style_shortlist.canonical_style`, unmapped dropped), tones must be in the `tone_style` Literal, weaknesses known dimension labels, checks known gate names. History spans **all users** of a brand (`creative_evals` has no user column; shared artifacts are expected under P3), and the brand match is exact after trimming and case-folding ("Acme" = "acme", but not "Acme Inc").

## Trend situation validator (`creative_agent/trend_situation.py`, 2026-10-11)

The grounded `trend_situation_pipeline` branch (START fan-out of `combined_research_pipeline`) drafts the trend's current moment; `validate_situation` decides by rule, never by the model's judgement, and **abstains** (phase forced to `unknown`, `timely_ok=False`, `abstain_reason` set) whenever it cannot verify the moment — unknown is the pre-feature behaviour, so the check fails safe.

| Draft | Result |
|---|---|
| no / invalid draft, or `TREND_STATE_ENABLED=false` | `unknown` ("no trend situation was drafted" / "disabled") |
| model chose `unknown` | `unknown` |
| upcoming / just happened with no parseable `event_start` | `unknown` |
| event more than `TREND_EVENT_WINDOW_DAYS` (7) from today | `unknown` |
| upcoming dated before today; just happened dated after today | `unknown` |
| just happened with an empty `outcome` | `unknown` |
| any cited id not in `state["sources"]`, or no cited source (upcoming / just happened) | `unknown` |
| just happened, a cited source's URL date (`/YYYY/MM/DD/`) < event − 1 day | `unknown` |
| `live` / `ongoing` / `evergreen` | phase kept, never timely (unknown ids → `unknown`) |
| `sensitive` | phase kept (unknown ids dropped, never downgraded), never timely; rendered for the brief with "Never reference this moment" |
| `confidence == "low"` | phase kept, not timely |
| `valid_until` already past | phase kept, not timely ("the moment has expired") |

"Today" is state `current_datetime` (seeded per run, `agent_common/clock.py`) or else the wall clock, in `current_timezone` / `TREND_TIMEZONE` (default America/New_York); a datetime with an offset is converted to that zone before taking its date (03:00 UTC on the 12th is the 11th in New York). `valid_until`: upcoming → 23:59:59 on the event day; just happened → `event_end` or `event_start` (a bare date = 23:59:59 that day) + `TIMELY_VALID_HOURS_AFTER` (48 h, 6–168). Grounded source URLs are usually `vertexaisearch` redirects without a date, so the URL-date rule rarely fires; it is a cheap guard for direct publisher URLs.

## Timely slot (`concept_guard.enforce_timely_cap`, `copy_gate.enforce_timely_copy_cap`, `temporal_terms.py`, 2026-10-11)

Switch: `trend_situation.timely_allowed(state, MAX_TIMELY_CONCEPTS)` = the validated moment is timely **and** the slot is on (default 1, 0–2). Without it the copy, concept and brief checks are exactly as before; the caps only clear stray `uses_trend_state` flags ("(Not timely: no validated current moment.)").

- **Cap:** the first `MAX_TIMELY_CONCEPTS` flagged concepts (concept order) / copies (copy order) keep their flag; later ones get "(Not timely: cap reached.)" appended to `timely_reason`. The concept cap runs in `ensure_trend_and_product_callback` right after the person-casting guard; the copy cap at the start of `copy_gate_decision`, which writes the capped `ad_copy_critique` back. The two orders can disagree, which the pairing check below then routes to the fixer.
- **Evergreen stays timeless:** an evergreen concept whose `image_generation_prompt` or headline, or an evergreen copy whose headline / body / caption / CTA, contains a `temporal_terms` hit is a deterministic issue (one bounded fix / revision round). The lexicon is whole-word, fold-insensitive and space/hyphen-interchangeable, and also matches explicit dates (month + day either way round, numeric `M/D`) and weekday names (singular only, so "Sundays" as a ritual passes). Idioms are removed first: "win-win", "lose weight", "weight loss", "lost and found", "miss a beat", "skin in the game", "24/7". "kick off" (verb) is fine, "kick-off"/"kickoff" are not. Extend `_IDIOMS` when a false positive appears.
- **Pairing:** a timely concept whose paired copy (`ad_copy_id` → `original_id`) is evergreen gets "make this concept evergreen".
- **Brief:** a non-empty `trend_bridge.timely_angle` needs a timely moment with the slot on; its result words must match the outcome (win words for a win, loss words for a loss, none before an upcoming event).

### 2026-10-08 gate changes (rater audit)

A live audit found the judge passed 16/16 creatives that a human failed 8/16 (trend unclear,
rendering defects incl. a mirrored guitar, off-brief, jokes about a real "Category 4"
hurricane). Three judge changes, all blocking:

- `no_visual_defects` (visual, "No visual defects"): the product is physically correct (right
  shape, parts in place and orientation, not mirrored/duplicated/melted/merged) and anatomy
  and geometry are normal; deliberate stylisation (cartoon, comic, collage) is exempt.
- `trend_risks_respected` (ad copy + visual, brief gate, "Respects trend risks"): the brief's
  `trend_bridge.risks` now reach the judge as a `Trend risks: a; b` line (omitted when there
  are none); the creative must not make light of, exploit or sensationalise those harms. No
  risks listed → pass; no brief → passes "no brief".
- `trend_motif_visible` is stricter: the motif must be prominent and recognisable at a glance
  as a reference to the campaign trend without reading any copy; tiny, background, generic
  ("any storm", "any phone screen") or merely implied motifs fail. **Loosened 2026-10-12**
  (`JUDGE_VERSION` `2026-10-12`): the motif must be visible (not tiny or hidden in the
  background) and recognisable as the trend to someone who follows it, without reading any
  copy; stylised, abstract or symbolic references count (e.g. a checkered finish-line
  pattern for a marathon, which a rater judged recognisable for "Chicago Marathon Results"
  while the judge had failed it); it fails only when the motif is absent, too small or
  hidden to notice, or so generic it could illustrate any trend.

Judge pass rates and `gates_pass_rate` before and after this change are **not comparable**.

### 2026-10-08 truthful briefs and copy (rater audit, PR 3)

The same audit (PRS SE CE24 Electric Guitar × "hurricane tracker") showed the brief writer
narrowing the product to a variant nobody gave ("SE CE 24 Standard Satin"), inventing claims
("guaranteed to stay in tune through 100 percent humidity") that reached the copy ("truly
indestructible"), and copy joking about "Category 4" humidity during a real storm. Fixed
upstream, before the judge:

- **Brief gate — exact product** (`brief_check.check_brief`): with a `target_product`, some
  mandatory must contain it as whole words (`text_match.contains_phrase`) or mention it per
  `text_match.mentions(m, product, brand=brand)` (so the brand + model-token rule, "PRS SE
  CE24", counts). Otherwise: `Name the product exactly as "<product>" in mandatories; do not
  add a variant, finish or model the user did not give.` (routed to the brief reviser).
- **Unsupported absolute claims** (`creative_agent/claims.py`, `ABSOLUTE_CLAIM_TERMS`:
  guarantee/guaranteed/guarantees, indestructible, unbreakable, bulletproof, risk-free, 100%,
  100 percent, never fails, never goes out of tune, lifetime). Whole-word, case/accent-blind,
  space/hyphen-insensitive ("risk free"), "100%" never matches a bare "100"; the idioms "of a
  lifetime" / "once in a lifetime" are exempt, and "never miss a moment" is not a claim. A term
  is allowed when the allowed text (the user's `key_selling_points` + the brief's mandatories)
  contains it or a family member (guarantee ↔ guaranteed, 100% ↔ 100 percent). Brief gate:
  proposition + reasons-to-believe claims → `Remove unsupported absolute claims (...) unless
  the user's selling points state them.` Copy gate: a **deterministic** issue over the
  headline, body, caption and CTA (so it gates and, if it survives, is a residual).
  A cited research source does not exempt a brief claim (the deterministic check cannot read
  the source); the prompts allow source-backed claims, so expect the reviser to soften them.
- **Trend risks** — the ad copy critic has a new `brief_checks` item `risks` (respects the
  brief's trend risks: never jokes about a real disaster's severity, victims, damage or
  official warnings). It is in `GATING_BRIEF_CHECKS`, so a failed self-check routes a revision
  like `proposition` / `mandatories`, and like them it is never a residual issue; the
  structural note now asks for proposition/mandatories/risks.
- **Prompts** (`creative_agent/prompts.py`): the brief writer uses the product name exactly as
  given and includes a mandatory naming it, makes no guarantees/absolute claims unless a cited
  source or the selling points state them, and for a real disaster/tragedy/crisis trend picks
  `light_touch`, lists the harms in `trend_bridge.risks` and never builds the idea on the
  harm. `TREND_RISKS_RULE` is shared by the drafter, critic and art director (visuals: no
  damage, victims, warning maps or storm graphics played for fun); `COPY_CLAIMS_RULE` by the
  drafter and critic.
